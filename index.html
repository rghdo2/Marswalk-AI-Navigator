NASA Data & Scientific Sources

Integration targets

Mars 2020 Perseverance landing-site context (Jezero Crater, ancient river delta). HiRISE and CTX imagery with derived digital terrain models, and MOLA elevation, all via NASA PDS and the USGS Astrogeology archive.

What is simulated here

Elevation, slopes, crater hazards, site coordinates, science scores and all timings in this page are generated demonstration data. Nothing here is real survey data.

Connecting real data

Real USGS CTX DTM and imagery are loaded via terrain-grid.js, dem-grid.js, and images-data.js.

Innovation, impact and what’s next

Explainable by design

Rule-based cost routing, described honestly. Every decision shows its inputs.

One source of truth

Map, planner and helmet share one route model, so they can never disagree.

Next

Real DTM slopes, rover and orbiter hazard updates, multi-day traverses, and voice guidance in the visor.
